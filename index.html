      FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

      Good questions. Clear answers.

      Is ScamShield AI really free?

      Yes. The current browser-based message and URL scanner is free. Proposed paid plans above are future ideas, not active subscriptions.

      Does it use a trained AI model?

      No. This initial version uses clearly defined rules. We call it ScamShield AI as a product name, but it does not use a machine-learning model or online threat-intelligence database.

      Does a low score mean a link is safe?

      No. A low score only means these basic rules did not spot many warning signs. Always verify independently before acting.

      Are my messages uploaded to a server?

      No. The scanner processes text directly in your browser. Avoid submitting private information regardless.

      Does the tool open or scan websites for malware?

      No. URL inspection analyzes the address text only. It does not visit the target or check live malware and phishing databases.
