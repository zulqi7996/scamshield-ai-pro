# Business and earning plan

## Positioning
Make common scam warning signs understandable to everyday users. Start with individuals and small businesses; avoid promising verified protection.

## Earning models (not yet implemented)
- **Free:** existing scanner builds trust, useful SEO content, optional donations later.
- **Pro concept, $4.99/month:** proposed batch scanning, history, and extra guidance. Requires privacy controls, account system, payment integration and real feature development.
- **Teams concept, $19/month:** proposed shared review workspace and export features. Requires reliable security, access management, multi-tenancy and audit logs.
- **B2B services:** paid onboarding or staff awareness materials, only after validating demand and ensuring customers understand limitations.

All prices are illustrative; no billing is active. Do not collect money until the proposed features exist and the appropriate supported payment provider is connected. Check your jurisdiction's rules and your provider's age/eligibility requirements.

## 30-day test
1. Week 1: interview 15 potential users; test examples, false positives, and comprehension.
2. Week 2: publish the free scanner and clear privacy/security explanation.
3. Week 3: share genuinely educational phishing-awareness content and ask for feedback.
4. Week 4: ask five small businesses whether they would pay for a safe, transparent team workflow. Record exact requirements.

## Success measures
- User comprehension of warnings
- False-positive and false-negative rates on a labeled, permissioned test set
- Number of people who voluntarily return
- Business interviews leading to paid pilot interest, not fabricated signups

## Marketing channels
SEO articles explaining common scam warning signs, short educational videos, LinkedIn posts, partnerships with IT trainers and small businesses. Avoid scare tactics and deceptive security claims.

## Cost notes
Static hosting can be free within provider limits. Dedicated email, APIs, threat feeds, payments and backend hosting may introduce costs.
